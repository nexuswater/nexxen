NEXXEN · AI Fintech on XRP Ledger hackathon · Oct 24–25, 2026

How a machine pays a machine

An AI buying agent purchases pay-per-call AI tools from independent seller agents over HTTP 402 (x402). Each call is paid with a signed RLUSD voucher, redeemed on XRPL EVM Testnet and settled to the seller through Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M). No wallet pop-up, no approval screen: the business sets a spending policy once.

4. Details the judges will ask about

Autonomy and safety

  • The agent has four tools: list_services, get_balance, buy_service, settle_payments. There is no approve tool; approval is the policy in config.
  • Before signing, the agent checks the 402 price against AP4M_MAX_PRICE (0.01 RLUSD) and the Channel cap against AP4M_MAX_CHANNEL_CAP.
  • Vouchers are EIP-712 typed data: domain AP4M Payment Ledger v1, chain 1449000, verifying contract the PaymentLedger; fields channelId, cumulativeAmount.
  • Private keys are read from environment variables and never sent to the AP4M service; only signatures leave the process.
  • Each seller runs its AI job in a separate model with zero tools, after redemption, so a malicious seller prompt cannot reach the buyer's wallet.

What is real

  • Every purchase is a real transaction on XRPL EVM Testnet, visible on Blockscout with a VoucherRedeemed log at the PaymentLedger address.
  • The 402 price is live: the store page asks each merchant for its challenge and shows the amount it returns.
  • The AP4M rails are the official hackathon kit: channels, redemption and settlement run on the kit's contracts and service.
  • The AI jobs are real model calls (summaries, translations, sentiment) and a real image endpoint.
  • Settlement to the merchant wallet is executed by the AP4M service; the merchant wallet's RLUSD transfers are on the explorer.

Why a machine has to pay here

  • Four sellers, four prices, hundreds of possible calls a day: a human approving each 0.001 RLUSD payment would cost more than the payment.
  • Vouchers are cumulative, so a Channel absorbs unlimited calls with one on-chain cap. That is the shape an agent economy needs: micro-payments with a macro limit.
  • The seller is itself an agent. It prices, verifies, redeems and delivers without an operator.
